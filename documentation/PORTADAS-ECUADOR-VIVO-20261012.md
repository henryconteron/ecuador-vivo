# Portadas de los espacios de trabajo

Generadas con la herramienta de imágenes integrada (skill `imagegen`), sin claves API, paquetes o descargas de modelos. Son ilustraciones decorativas, no mapas ni resultados científicos. Los PNG de origen se conservaron en la carpeta de imágenes generadas de Codex; la aplicación usa únicamente las versiones WebP locales de 900 × 600 px, 160.744 bytes entre las tres.

Archivos usados: `production/video_studio/workspace_frontend/art/sig-cover.webp`, `studio-cover.webp` y `resources-cover.webp`.

## Prompt SIG

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: decorative cover for the SIG workspace of Ecuador Vivo, a personal Ecuadorian geoscience application. Create a polished landscape editorial 3D illustration: a small Andean mountain and Amazonian river landscape resting on three separated thin cartographic glass layers, delicate contour engravings, one clean location pin. This is conceptual artwork, not a real map or measurement. Palette midnight blue #0c1b24, teal #57dec8, stone-blue #7eafc1 with tiny warm sand details. Dark navy backdrop, art centered on the right two-thirds with generous margins, soft studio lighting, precise crafted surfaces, crisp detail, restrained elegant composition. No text, letters, logos, UI screenshot, real geographic boundary, watermark, extra people. Landscape 3:2.

## Prompt Studio

Create a polished landscape editorial 3D illustration: a floating cinematic composition frame showing an abstract Andean mountain silhouette and three thin timeline strips beneath, with a simple play triangle in polished turquoise glass. Keep it a conceptual video-making artwork rather than a screenshot; NO numerical charts or data. Palette midnight navy #0c1b24, turquoise #57dec8, icy blue #7eafc1 and a little warm amber. Dark navy backdrop, art centered on the right two-thirds with generous margins. Beautiful crafted matte and translucent surfaces, crisp details and soft studio lighting. No text, labels, logos, fake menus, watermark or people. Landscape 3:2.

## Prompt Recursos

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: decorative cover for Ecuador Vivo Recursos, an educational diagrams and animation creator. Create a polished landscape editorial 3D illustration of an elegant creative toolkit: three floating slim composition cards, one with a simple layered rock block, one with a water droplet, one with an orbital sphere, connected by a single curved turquoise arrow. Conceptual visual communication artwork, not empirical scientific data. Dark midnight navy #0c1b24 backdrop, turquoise #57dec8 and cool blue #7eafc1, tiny restrained amber accent. Art on right two-thirds with generous margins, crisp crafted matte paper and translucent glass, soft studio lighting. No text, letters, numbers, logos, interface screenshot, watermark or people. Landscape 3:2.

## Tratamiento posterior

Reducción proporcional de cada imagen y conversión a WebP con Pillow existente. No se añadieron textos rasterizados: títulos y botones son elementos reales de la aplicación. Las ilustraciones no se insertan automáticamente en los videos ni modifican sus datos. No confundir la estética tridimensional de estas portadas con un editor científico 3D ya implementado.
